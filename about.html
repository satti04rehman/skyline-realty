Our story

Built on trust, driven by results.

For over two decades, Skyline Realty has been the name Pakistani families trust when it comes to buying, selling and renting property.

Who we are

From a small Lahore office to Pakistan's trusted name in real estate.

Founded in 2006 by a team of property professionals who saw a gap between what buyers needed and what the market offered, Skyline Realty started with a single office in DHA Phase 5, Lahore.

Today we operate in 10 cities across Pakistan — from the bustling streets of Karachi's Clifton to the planned avenues of Islamabad's DHA. Our team of 45+ agents and consultants has helped over 3,500 families find homes, invest in plots and close commercial deals.

We believe the best property transaction is one where both buyer and seller walk away satisfied. That principle guides every deal we handle.

Our values

What drives every decision we make.

◎

Integrity first

We never recommend a property we wouldn't buy ourselves. Every listing is verified for legal standing, ownership history and structural quality before it reaches you.

✧

Client over commission

If a deal isn't right for you, we'll tell you. Our reputation is built on long-term relationships, not one-time transactions.

✎

Market transparency

We share real market data, comparable sales and honest valuations so you can make informed decisions without pressure.

✆

Personal attention

Every client gets a dedicated consultant who knows your requirements, follows up regularly and is available when you need them.

◈

Community roots

We invest in the communities we serve — sponsoring local events, supporting education initiatives and mentoring new entrants in the property industry.

★

Continuous improvement

We regularly train our team on market trends, legal changes and client service best practices to stay ahead of the curve.

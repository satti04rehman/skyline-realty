Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common queries.

How do I schedule a property visit?
Simply message us on WhatsApp or fill out the contact form above. A consultant will get in touch within a few hours to arrange a free site visit at your preferred time. We cover all major housing societies in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad.
Are your listed properties legally verified?
Yes. Every property listed by Skyline Realty undergoes a thorough legal check including ownership verification, society approval status, encumbrance certificate and possession status. We never list a property we haven't verified ourselves.
What are your commission rates?
Our commission structure is transparent and agreed upon in writing before any transaction begins. For residential sales, we typically charge 2% of the sale price. For rentals, we charge one month's rent as our fee. All terms are clearly communicated upfront — no hidden charges.
Do you help overseas Pakistanis?
Absolutely. We have a dedicated overseas client desk that handles virtual tours, video consultations, power of attorney guidance and complete transaction management. Many of our clients in the Gulf, UK and USA have purchased properties through us without visiting Pakistan.
Can I sell my property through Skyline Realty?
Yes. Contact us for a free property evaluation. We'll assess your property's market value, recommend a competitive listing price and handle all marketing, buyer screening, negotiation and paperwork on your behalf.
Which cities do you operate in?
We currently operate in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot and Abbottabad. Our head office is in DHA Phase 5, Lahore.
